# Blobfish Survivors — Complete Reference

Every number below is pulled directly from the current game code, not from memory — and the weapon/passive tables below have also been checked against the running game level by level, so this reflects what's shipped right now. **Version 5 (2026-10-25)** changed a lot: progress now runs through the areas (Shop tiers open with the bosses of the deeper areas, Blobis are unlocked and then bought), every boss drops a treasure chest, Second Chance is a button you press, a late-game flood ends long runs, lava burns enemies, champions and bosses have new detailed, animated art (champion bosses even more, and they are bigger) while normal enemies and the backgrounds keep their simple look, and online play has shared loot and a PvP mode. Percentages and damage values are shown *before* your character's damage multiplier, Shop bonuses and the Schlagkraft passive are applied, except where noted. **Version 6 (2026-10-30)** is about online play and polish: the game is now called **Blobfish Survivors** (English spelling, to match "Survivors"), it speaks **German, English and Ukrainian** (toggle on the start menu, Section 16), online rooms have a **Lead Fish** who can call the swarm to him, PvP is **team Blau against team Rot** with far fewer enemies, champions are harder (and shoot), "Zum Menü" saves the run, and there are new sounds, smoother frame pacing on fast phone screens, better multi-touch and several online fixes. **Version 7 (2026-10-31)** adds the PvP mode **Free-for-all** (free-for-all) next to team PvP, rebuilds the online menus as short steps with two choices each, replaces the crowded points line in online play with a small labelled **score card**, keeps arrows, labels, toasts and the player list from overlapping, shows **progress bars** for every achievement, gives the game-over screen one clear next step, and reworks the **sounds** (Section 14). The TurboWarp Lite version (Lite 4) has the single-player rules of versions 5 to 7 but not the 7.2 bosses; Section 15 lists the differences.

**Unlock, then buy.** At the start only the Standard Blobi is playable. When you meet a Blobi's condition it becomes *unlocked*: a message tells you, and the menu shows a **NEUER BLOBI** badge until you have looked at it. Then you buy it in the Blobi selection with banked pearls (the card shows the price; the first tap asks, the second tap buys). The prices add up to 4,700 pearls. Saves from before version 5 keep every Blobi they already had, and players who unlocked Lucky Blobi under the earlier level-15 rule keep it.

The character-select cards show the real, current numbers (including any Shop bonuses), and the stage cards show their difficulty multiplier and boss. These are *base* stats. Shop upgrades (Section 9) add flat/percentage bonuses on top of whichever character you pick, every run.

Turbo, Panzer, and Glücks are drawn from the side, so instead of spinning all the way around (which would flip them upside-down), they mirror horizontally once you're heading more than 90° away from their natural facing, and only tilt within that ±90° window. Standard is the only one drawn from directly above, so it turns freely in a full circle.

## 2. Areas (Stages)

| Stage | Difficulty multiplier | Boss | Unlocked by | Pearls per kill | Boss treasure (1st) | Area mechanic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coral Reef (Coral Reef) | 1.0 | Shark King | Available from the start | 10% chance × 1 pearl — **×1** | 25 | None — a harmless starter stage |
| Kelp Forest (Kelp Forest) | 1.35 | Giant Kraken | Defeating the Shark King | 25% × 1 — **×2.5** | 60 | Kelp patches slow you down |
| Deep-Sea Trench (Deep Trench) | 1.75 | Sea Monster | Defeating the Giant Kraken | 20% × 2 — **×4** | 110 | Darkness — you only see near you |
| Volcano Gorge (Volcanic Vent) | 2.2 | Lava Titan | Defeating the Sea Monster | 20% × 3 — **×6** | 180 | Lava pools |

The difficulty multiplier affects how fast regular enemies gain HP/damage over time and how quickly the spawn rate ramps up (see Section 3) — a run in Volcano Gorge gets harder much faster than the same elapsed time in Coral Reef.

**Pearls per kill:** the chance that a regular enemy drops a pearl, and how many pearls that pearl is worth, depend on the area. The bold factor is the expected pearls per kill compared with the Coral Reef (0.1 → 0.25 → 0.4 → 0.6 per kill). The boss treasure (Section 4), champion chests (Section 5), Elite pearls and the pearls for a maxed level-up all scale with the area too, so a deeper area pays several times more for the same time played.

**Why the deeper areas matter (version 5):** the Shop's higher tiers only open once you have beaten the boss of a deeper area (Section 9), Turbo- and Armor Blobi are unlocked by those bosses (Section 1), and the deeper areas pay far more pearls. Measured with a bot (`webtest/economy_measure.js`, 22-minute runs): an immortal bot brings home about 500–600 pearls in the Coral Reef without Shop upgrades and 1,000–2,500 in the Volcano Gorge with tier 2; everything in the Shop and all Blobis cost 23,935 pearls together.

**Unlocking:** every stage after the Coral Reef opens once you defeat the boss of the previous one (in any run, with any character). Locked stage cards show a 🔒 and what to beat. Saves that already had progress when this system arrived keep all four stages open.

**Area mechanics in detail:**
- **Coral Reef — nothing.** No hazards; it's meant for learning the game.
- **Kelp Forest — kelp patches.** Fixed patches of swaying kelp (radius 70–130 units; about 4 in every 10 map cells of 380 × 380 units contain one, and none sit near your starting point). While you're inside one, your movement speed is ×0.6 (−40%). This multiplies with the Squid ink slow, so both together leave you at ×0.33. Enemies aren't slowed.
- **Deep-Sea Trench — darkness.** Everything outside a lit circle around you is dark. The circle's radius is half the shorter screen side, clamped to 230–420 units (and gently pulsing); it is fully clear in the inner 40% and fades to 90% dark at 130% of that radius. XP crystals, pearls, hearts and chests (with the boss chest's light beam), enemy shots, bosses and the Anglerfish's lure stay visible in the dark. Version 7.6: pickups are drawn there with their real pictures; before they were plain coloured circles (a tester saw balls instead of crystals).
- **Volcano Gorge — lava pools.** From 0:12 on, lava erupts around you on a timer: every `max(3.2, 7 − seconds/90)` seconds, `min(3, 1 + ⌊seconds/200⌋)` pools (radius 78) appear. The first is placed where you're heading (0.7 s ahead of your movement), the others 110–340 units away in random directions. Each pool shows a glowing warning circle with cracks for 1.5 s, then burns for 3.5 s as molten lava with a soft glowing edge (it darkens as it cools down). Standing in active lava hurts about once every 0.5 s (your i-frames) for `9 × (1 + minutes × 0.10 × 2.2)` damage (× the late-game flood factor after 15:00, Section 3) — Panzerung reduces it and a charged Shield blocks one tick. Lava isn't a shot, so the Bubble Ring can't stop it. **Lava also burns enemies** standing in it: 30 % of their max HP per second (Elites 15 %, champions 5 %, bosses 1.2 %; the Lava Titan is immune), so luring enemies through a pool is a real tactic; enemies killed by lava drop their loot as usual. The Lava Titan adds a few lava pools of its own in its last phase (Section 4).

**Idle animation (version 7.4):** the normal enemies move a little, so they no longer look frozen: the jellyfish's and octopus's tentacles wave, the sharks', eels' and piranhas' fins flap, the crabs' claws open and close a little, the sea urchin's spikes breathe and turn slightly, the anglerfish's lure swings and pulses. Eight pre-drawn frames per type and size (sizes are grouped in halves), played at 0.7 (tentacles), 1.1 (fins), 0.9 (claws), 0.45 (spikes) and 0.8 (lure) loops per squash cycle (`BLOB_FRAMES`, `BLOB_ACC_RATE`); the white hit flash stays one frame; off with the system setting "reduce motion". Champions keep their own detailed art.

**Which enemies appear where** (listed in the order they're introduced: only the first type spawns at the start, and one more type joins every 25 seconds, so the full roster is out after 100 s in the Riff and after 150 s in the other stages; each unlocked type is then picked with equal probability):
- **Riff:** Jellyfish, Small Shark, Crab, Anglerfish, Piranha
- **Kelp:** Jellyfish, Small Shark, Crab, Moray Eel, Squid, Piranha, Sea Urchin
- **Graben:** Small Shark, Moray Eel, Squid, Crab, Anglerfish, Sea Urchin, Giant Crab
- **Vulkan:** Squid, Moray Eel, Crab, Piranha, Anglerfish, Sea Urchin, Giant Crab

**Time-based scaling** (applied at the moment each enemy spawns):
- HP multiplier: `1 + (minutes elapsed × 0.24 × stage difficulty)`
- Damage multiplier: `1 + (minutes elapsed × 0.10 × stage difficulty)`

**Spawning:** starts at 1 enemy per wave, gains +1 to the wave size every ~100 seconds (capped at 4 per wave), and the gap between waves shrinks from 1.2s down to a floor of 0.45s as the stage difficulty pushes it down over time (`max(0.45, 1.2 − minutes × 0.055 × stage difficulty)`).

**While a boss is alive** the arena is calmer so you can focus on the duel: each wave brings only half as many regular enemies (rounded up, at least 1), the gap between waves is 1.4× longer, no Elites are created and the Piranha-Ring waits until the boss is dead. (The boss's own helpers are separate, see Section 4.)

**The 70-enemy cap:** at most 70 regular enemies are alive at once. At the cap, a new spawn never deletes anything you can see — it *replaces the farthest enemy that is outside the screen*, and if every enemy is on-screen the spawn is simply skipped. Bosses, elites and champions are never replaced, and a champion appears even when the cap is reached (Section 5).

**Piranha schools and rings:** whenever the spawner picks a Piranha it spawns a whole school of `4 + min(5, ⌊seconds/90⌋)` packed together (4 at the start, 9 from 7:30 on). On stages that have Piranhas (Riff, Kelp, Vulkan), a **Piranha-Ring** hits at 2:00 and then every 100 s (postponed while a boss is alive): 12 Piranhas appear evenly spaced on a circle just outside the screen and close in all at once, announced by a banner.

**Elites:** from 3:00 on, roughly every 55 s the next regular spawn (a Piranha school never counts) becomes an **Elite**: gold ring, ×3 HP, ×1.25 damage, ×1.3 size, ×4 XP, and +3 extra pearls when it dies (each worth the area's pearl value).

**Pearl drops:** a regular enemy drops a pearl with the area's drop chance — Riff 10%, Kelp 25%, Graben 20%, Vulkan 20% — and each pearl is worth the area's pearl value: 1 in Riff and Kelp, 2 in Graben, 3 in Vulkan (Section 2). Bosses drop a treasure chest (Section 4). Luck adds +10% per point to every pearl you collect (Section 7).

**The late-game flood (from 15:00):** a banner says "🌊 Die Flut steigt!", and from then on every minute makes new enemies tougher: HP × 1.22 and damage × 1.12 per minute past 15:00 (compounding, on top of the normal time scaling: × 2.7 HP and × 1.8 damage at 20:00, × 7.3 and × 3.1 at 25:00). Bosses get × 1.105 HP (the square root) and × 1.12 damage per minute, lava × 1.12 damage. The wave size cap grows by one every 2 minutes (5 at 17:00, 6 at 19:00, …) and an Elite comes every 30 s instead of every 55 s. Sooner or later the flood ends every run — the longer you last, the more pearls you take home.

**Look:** normal enemies keep the simple, round blob look: a coloured blob with a face and one feature per type (tentacles, a fin, claws, spikes, a lure). They gently squash as they move, and a hit makes them flash white. The Anglerfish's lure gives a small, soft light in the dark of the Deep-Sea Trench (much dimmer since version 5). The detailed, animated drawings are reserved for **champions** (Section 5) and **bosses** (Section 4), so a special enemy stands out at a glance. The collision circle is the enemy's radius from the table.

**Piles of orbs:** XP orbs and pearls that lie far away from you are merged so that huge piles don't slow the game down, and **nothing is ever lost**. Orbs outside the screen simply combine invisibly into one bigger orb (bigger orbs are drawn slightly larger, up to radius 12). If more than 200 orbs are lying around, the ones that are on-screen but more than 60 units beyond your pickup radius no longer vanish: they slowly drift into the nearest orb of their patch and add up when they arrive. As soon as you swim within pickup range, a drifting orb becomes individually collectible again. Hearts never merge.

## 4. Bosse

### Version 7.2: six bosses per area, every boss with its own attacks

**Which boss comes.** Timing is unchanged (first at 5:00, then 4 minutes after each kill); only the species changes. The 1st boss of a run is one of the area's three *Einstieg* bosses (E); from the 2nd on they come from a shuffled bag of all six (the first is already out of it), no repeat until the bag is empty, then it is shuffled again. Scaling keeps counting *encounters*, not species. Online, the room anchor keeps the room's bag and sends the species with the world-boss call. (Kelp Forest, Deep-Sea Trench and Volcano Gorge still have only their one boss until their new bosses are built.)

**Gates.** Beating **any** boss of an area opens the next area, the Shop tiers and Turbo-/Armor Blobi ("Besiege einen Boss im Kelp Forest."). "Boss Conqueror" now means one boss in each of the four areas.

**Coral Reef: easier, with fewer extras.** Riff bosses have **20 % less HP and damage** than the tables, every boss warning lasts **0.3 s longer**, repeats add **+10 % HP** (not 15 %), waits between attacks shrink to at most **×0.85** (not ×0.7). Tier 1 uses only the signature move and the heavy shot; the burst joins at tier 2, the second move at tier 3, and **tier 3 only from the 3rd boss** of a run. One helper less per phase, none in phase 2 on the first boss. Riff champions and champion bosses only get **Swift, Armored, Splitting** (bosses: Swift, Armored), and a Riff champion boss starts angry (phase 2) only **from the 4th boss**. Elites start at **4:00** (others 3:00), the Piranha-Ring at **3:00** (others 2:00). Rewards are unchanged.

The radius is the boss's collision circle (10 % larger since version 5); the boss is drawn another 12 % larger than that, so it towers over every normal enemy. A champion boss is another 15 % larger (Section 5).

**Bosses recur.** The first one shows up 5 minutes into a run (with a 3-second warning banner). Defeat it, and the *next* one — same species, tied to your stage — arrives exactly 4 minutes later, and every subsequent appearance is stronger than the last. This can continue indefinitely as long as you keep surviving.

**How strong a boss is.** Bosses scale with *you*, so they stay a real fight no matter how fast you level:
- HP: `base HP × (1 + (stage difficulty − 1) × 0.5) × (1 + 0.15 × earlier appearances) × level factor`
- Level factor: `(your level ÷ 21)^1.3`, measured at the moment the boss appears and limited to 0.7 – 6. That is ×0.7 up to level 16, ×1.0 at level 21, ×1.6 at level 30, ×2.3 at level 40, ×3.9 at level 60 and the cap of ×6 from level 84 on.
- Damage: `base damage × (1 + 0.2 × earlier appearances)` — so ×1.0, ×1.2, ×1.4, … on the 1st, 2nd, 3rd appearance.

**HUD.** While a boss is alive, its name, a `×N` mark for repeat appearances and its current phase (`Phase 1–3`) appear above a red HP bar with notches at 66% and 33%. If the boss is off-screen, a red arrow at the screen edge points toward it.

**Phases.** A boss fights in three phases: **Phase 1** above 66% HP, **Phase 2** from 66% down to 33%, **Phase 3** below 33%. Each time it enters a new phase:
- A banner announces it ("⚠ … wird wütend!", in Phase 3 "… tobt vor Wut!").
- The boss **roars** for 1.1 s: it stands completely still and fires nothing extra, with expanding rings around it. Then it releases a ring of 12 shots (16 in Phase 3, 60% of its damage, ordinary shots).
- It **calls its helpers** (table above): 3 in Phase 2 and 5 in Phase 3, plus 1 more on the 2nd appearance and 2 more from the 3rd appearance on. Helpers are normal enemies with the usual time scaling and obey the 70-enemy cap.
- Its next signature move can't come sooner than 2.2 s after the roar began.

**Attack tier.** What a boss does depends on its *tier*: `tier = min(3, phase + appearance − 1)`. On its first appearance, tier = phase. A returning boss starts with the attacks of the next phase — the 2nd appearance already fights at tier 2 in Phase 1, and from the 3rd appearance on a boss always uses tier 3. The tier also sets how fast it is and how often it attacks:

| Tier | Boss walking speed | Wait between attacks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ×1.0 | ×1.0 |
| 2 | ×1.1 | ×0.85 |
| 3 | ×1.2 | ×0.7 |

On top of that, each earlier appearance shortens the waits by another 8% (this extra shortening stops at ×0.7 of the waiting time). The result for the signature move: 1st appearance ×1.00 / ×0.85 / ×0.70 in Phases 1/2/3, 2nd appearance ×0.78 in Phase 1 and ×0.64 later, 3rd ×0.59, 4th ×0.53, 5th and later ×0.49.

None of them can be stopped by the Bubble Ring (only the *shots* mentioned further down can), a charged Shield absorbs one hit, Panzerung reduces the damage, and the usual 0.5 s of i-frames apply. ## 5. Champions

A **champion** is a rare, much tougher version of a normal enemy with one special trait (two late in a run). It is worth hunting: it drops a treasure chest. Champions sit one clear step above the gold-ringed Elites, which stay as they are. Version 6 made them clearly harder (more HP, harder hits, a little faster, and they shoot); the TurboWarp Lite version has the same champions since Lite 4.

**When they come**
- The first champion appears at **4:00**, then one every **120–150 s** (random).
- Never during a boss fight (also not during the 3-second boss warning). After a boss dies, the next champion waits at least 20 s.
- At most **1** at a time before 15:00, up to **2** from **15:00** on.
- It is a random type from the area's current spawn rotation (Section 3), except **Piranha** (too small) and **Sea Urchin** (can't move). A champion appears even when the 70-enemy cap is full, and the cap never removes one.
- A message announces it ("👑 Champion erschienen: Gepanzerter Giant Crab") with a fanfare, and while it is off-screen a small arrow in its trait colour points to it.

**How strong**
- HP: `normal HP at that moment × 12 × level factor` (×8 until version 5). The level factor is the bosses' one (Section 4): `(your level ÷ 21)^1.3`, limited to 0.7 – 6. Online, the level is the average of the players near the champion, and like every new enemy it gets **+50 % HP for every other player nearby** (Section 13). Online with others in the room, champions come on the room's schedule (Section 13).
- Damage ×1.7, size ×1.5, speed ×1.1, XP ×10 (until version 5: damage ×1.4, normal speed).
- **Fan shot (version 6):** every **3.5 s** (the first after 1.75–3.5 s), when you're within 620 units and it isn't dashing, it fires a fan of **3 shots** at its target, 0.26 rad (about 15°) apart, at 175 units/s, each dealing **55 % of its touch damage**; the shots have its trait colour and live 3.2 s. A Shield blocks them like any enemy shot. Champion bosses don't use it (they have their own attacks).
- Examples (single-player): a Crab champion at 4:00 in the Riff at level 15 has about 428 HP (a normal Crab: 51); a Moray Eel champion at 10:00 in the Kelp Forest at level 30 has about 1,050 HP; a Giant Crab champion at 15:00 in the Deep-Sea Trench at level 45 has about 16,500 HP and hits for about 92.

**Look:** unlike normal enemies, a champion is drawn in full detail and animated (since version 5; since 7.14 with 12 to 24 pictures per loop, depending on the speed of the movement, so it looks smooth): the Jellyfish's bell pulses and its threads drift, the Small Shark, Anglerfish, Moray Eel and Piranha are drawn from the side and face the way they swim (tilting a little up or down), the Crab and Giant Crab walk and snap their claws, the Anglerfish's lure pulses, the Sea Urchin's spines sway, the Moray Eel's spotted body snakes behind it, the Squid waves its arms and the Piranha snaps; a hit flashes it white. Luck applies (+10 % per point). Chests are never merged with other pickups; online every player near the champion gets one.

**Champion bosses:** from the **3rd boss encounter** of a run on, the boss arrives as a **champion boss**:
- one trait: Swift (the boss walks 35 % faster), Armored, Vampire or Leader;
- **"wütend"**: it roars as soon as it arrives and fights in Phase 2 from the start (it can still reach Phase 3); the message reads "👑 Champion-Boss: … – wütend von Anfang an!" and the HUD name gets a 👑;
- **15 % bigger** than the normal boss (collision circle and picture, since version 5; online the other players see the same size);
- HP ×1.3 on top of the normal boss scaling (Section 4);
- **look (version 5):** a double outline (gold outside, its trait colour inside) and an aura in its trait colour; under it a glowing seal with two rings, one dashed and one with twelve runes, turning in opposite directions; seven sparks circle it (passing in front of and behind it, with short tails), embers in its colour rise around it, and a shockwave ring pulses out every 3.2 s. The three without a crown of their own get a floating state crown above them. In the Deep-Sea Trench the seal, sparks and crown stay visible in the dark, and the corpse keeps the jewellery;
- reward: the usual heart and a **double Boss Treasure** (twice the pearls of Section 4) that also holds a **free upgrade card**: a normal level-up choice ("🎁 Schatztruhe!") that costs no XP. If a level-up and a free card are waiting at the same time, the level-up comes first.

**Achievements:** Champion Hunter (defeat 25 champions, +60 pearls) and Crown Breaker (defeat a champion boss, +120), see Section 10.

**How much they add (measured in version 3, before the version 5 economy).** Bot runs of 20 minutes with and without champions, same seeds: in the Coral Reef the bot defeated about 5.4 champions and 0.8 champion bosses per run, which made up about 9–10 % of the run's pearls; in the Deep-Sea Trench about 2 champions, 7 %. No measurable extra levels (the differences stayed within the normal run-to-run spread). Human players who hunt champions get a bit more. The target was 10–15 % of the pearls and at most 1–2 extra levels by 20:00. Version 6 raised `CHAMPION_CHEST` from 12 to 15 together with making champions harder, so hunting them stays worth it.

## 6. Waffen (Weapons)

Every weapon can be leveled 1–5 by picking it (or picking it again) on level-up. Damage figures already include the "+level" scaling but **not** your character's damage multiplier, the Shop damage bonus or the Schlagkraft passive — multiply by all of them to get your real numbers. The level-up cards do that for you and show the final values.

**Reach and targeting:** projectile weapons only fire at enemies within `560 × screen scale` units, where the screen scale is `min(2.4, max(1, longer screen side ÷ 850))` — 1.0 on phones, about 1.6 on a 1366-px laptop, about 2.3 on a 1920-px monitor (capped at 2.4). Projectile speed and lifetime are both stretched by √scale so the shots really reach that far. At scale 1 the reach is about 588 units (Bubble Shot), 616 (Harpoon) and 585 (Coral Spear).

### Bubble Shot (Bubble Shot) — starting weapon
Fires at the nearest enemy in targeting range. The bubble flies in a straight line to where the target was at the moment of firing — it does *not* home in, so fast movers can slip past it. Extra shots fan out about 10° apart. Base cooldown 0.85s.

| Level | Damage | Shots fired |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10.4 | 1 |
| 2 | 13.8 | 1 |
| 3 | 17.2 | 2 |
| 4 | 20.6 | 2 |
| 5 | 24.0 | 3 |

### Bubble Ring (Bubble Ring)
Orbiting bubbles that damage anything they touch (0.3s cooldown per individual enemy) **and destroy ordinary enemy shots on contact** (small deflect burst + sound; it does not heal you). Does not stop bosses' heavy red shots.

| Level | Bubbles | Damage/hit | Orbit radius |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 9 | 59 |
| 2 | 4 | 12 | 66 |
| 3 | 6 | 15 | 73 |
| 4 | 8 | 18 | 80 |
| 5 | 10 | 21 | 87 |

**Wear and popping (from ring level 4).** Blocking shots wears the bubbles down — but the ring is safe while you build it up, and you always get the chance to repair it:
- **Levels 1–3: bubbles never pop.** From **level 4** (the second-to-last upgrade) on, every ordinary enemy shot that a bubble blocks counts as one hit on *that* bubble. Heavy boss shots pass through and don't count, and neither does touching enemies.
- A worn bubble turns paler and reddish, shakes and shows cracks (cracks from about 40% wear, more after 70%). A small `⭕ 8/8` counter in the HUD shows your bubbles (it turns warning-colored when some are missing).
- After **12 blocked shots** a bubble pops. Three rules keep this fair: at most **one bubble pops every 50 seconds**; after a pop **no further bubble can pop until a level-up has been offered** (a bubble that is "due" simply waits, one hit short of popping); and when a bubble pops, all the others are relieved to at most half wear (6 hits).
- A banner tells you: the next level-up will let you refill the ring. That level-up **always contains the ring card**, so you never have to hunt for it — but you're free to pick something else; the next pop is then at least 50 s away, and the card is guaranteed again right after that pop (it can also turn up in any level-up as one of the normal random cards).
- **Ring level 4 → 5:** the normal upgrade card is the repair — a new level always comes with a full, undamaged ring.
- **Ring level 5 (maxed):** the card becomes **„More bubbles“** (the "buy more" card). It is free, restores every missing bubble and repairs all cracks (`Blasen 8 → 10 · alle Risse repariert`). It only appears while at least one bubble is missing, so it never clutters the level-up screen at full health.
- Popping is part of the mid-run snapshot, so a resumed run keeps its bubble wear.

### Ink Cloud (Ink Cloud)
Pulses from a point trailing 34 units *behind* your current facing direction (not centered on you), rendered as a soft, multi-tinted, drifting cloud. Base cooldown 1.2s. Deals **percentage of the target's max HP**, and slows anything it hits by 35% for 1.5s. Only a fraction of its damage applies to bosses (15 %) and champions (40 %, since version 5) so it can't trivialize them. It ignores your damage multiplier, the Shop damage bonus and Schlagkraft — the percentages below are exactly what it deals, which is why it keeps pace with enemy HP scaling. In PvP it hits other players for a flat `(6 + 3 × level) × your damage` instead (then the PvP rules of Section 13 apply).

When the bonus card appears, the level-up screen says so.

There are no healing pickups lying around the map (a pickup like that existed briefly and was removed for being too strong). The rest of the HP you regain mid-run comes from Regeneration (continuous, on top of the base 1 HP/min), the one-off +20 heal when you pick Lebenskraft, and the **heart every boss drops** (+40% of your max HP, Section 4); the Shield blocks a hit but doesn't heal.

## 8. Spezialmechaniken (How defense works, tied together)

1. **Ordinary enemy shots** (Anglerfish, the Squid ink, and the boss's radial bursts, roar rings, Suction rings, fans, tooth shots and ink ring) — blockable by the Bubble Ring on contact. From ring level 4, every block wears the bubble that took it (Section 6).
2. **Heavy boss shots** (the big glowing red ones) — ignore the Ring entirely. Dodge them or have a charged Shield.
3. **Contact damage** from touching an enemy — reduced by Panzerung, avoidable with i-frames (0.5s after any hit) or a charged shield.
4. **Hazards and boss moves** (lava zones, tentacle slams and the whip, the Magma Shockwave, the Shark King's charge, the Suction pull) — not shots, so the Ring can't touch them. They are all telegraphed and avoided by moving; Panzerung reduces them, a charged Shield absorbs one hit, and i-frames apply (which is why standing in lava hurts about every half second).
5. **Sustain** — the base 1 HP per minute, Regeneration (continuous), the one-off +20 heal from picking Lebenskraft and the heart from every boss restore HP; Shield blocks a hit without healing; Second Chance (Shop) is the only revive.

**Leveling curve:** the XP needed to leave level *N* is `⌊6 + N × 4.6⌋` (rounded down), so it grows roughly linearly (a bit under 5 XP more needed per level) — for example 10 XP to go from level 1 to 2, 52 XP from 10 to 11 and 121 XP from 25 to 26. Once every weapon and passive is maxed (and no bubble is missing), a level-up gives pearls instead of a card: 3 × the area's pearl value (3 in Riff and Kelp, 6 in Graben, 9 in Vulkan).

**Level-up cards:** each card says exactly what changes, e.g. `Stufe 2 → 3: Schaden 13,8 → 17,2 · Blasen 1 → 2`. A new weapon or passive shows `Neu:` with its level-1 values. The ring's refill card is called „More bubbles“ and shows `Blasen 8 → 10 · alle Risse repariert`. Numbers already include your damage multiplier and cooldown bonuses. If you reload the page on the level-up screen, the same cards are restored.

## 9. Shop (Permanent Upgrades, bought with banked pearls)

Every upgrade has its own price per level, and the higher levels are **gated by the bosses of the deeper areas**: tier 0 is open from the start, tier 1 needs the Giant Kraken (Kelp Forest) beaten, tier 2 the Sea Monster (Deep-Sea Trench), tier 3 the Lava Titan (Volcano Gorge) — once, in any run. So the Coral Reef alone can never max you out.

| Upgrade | Effect/level | Lv1 | Lv2 | Lv3 | Lv4 | Lv5 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bigger Health Pool | +10 starting max HP | 40 | 110 | 380 *(tier 1)* | 900 *(tier 2)* | 2,000 *(tier 3)* | 3,430 |
| Better Fins | +3% starting speed | 35 | 95 | 330 *(1)* | 800 *(2)* | 1,800 *(3)* | 3,060 |
| Sharper Spikes | +4% weapon damage | 55 | 150 | 500 *(1)* | 1,200 *(2)* | 2,600 *(3)* | 4,505 |
| Pearl Magnet | +22 pickup radius (base becomes 70 + 22×level) | 30 | 80 | 280 *(1)* | 700 *(2)* | 1,500 *(3)* | 2,590 |
| Lucky Charm | +1 starting Luck (more pearls, more often a 4th upgrade card) | 250 | 1,200 *(2)* | 3,000 *(3)* | — | — | 4,450 |
| Second Chance | Once per run, a button that revives you at 50 % HP | 1,200 *(1)* | — | — | — | — | 1,200 |

Everything costs **19,235 pearls**, plus **4,700** for the three Blobis (Section 1). The Shop shows the three gate bosses as chips at the top (✔ once beaten, 🔒 until then), each upgrade's levels as pips (locked ones dimmed), the price of the next level, and for a gated level "🔒 Nächste Stufe: besiege …". When you beat the boss that opens a tier, a message tells you. Saves from before version 5 keep every level they already bought.

**How Second Chance works (version 5):** a one-time, permanent purchase (1,200 pearls, after you have beaten the Giant Kraken). Every run then starts with the revive armed, shown as a green heart (💚) next to your HP numbers. The first time you'd die, **the game stops and a bar appears with 10 seconds to decide**: press **„💚 Second Chance“** (or Space / Enter) and you're back at 50 % max HP with 2 s of invulnerability and a revive flash, while enemies within 320 units are blasted back out to about 340 units (bosses are shoved 140 units) and every enemy shot, lava pool, tentacle slam and shockwave is wiped. The bar also says what is at stake (version 7: "Sonst ist dein Lauf vorbei: Level 12 · 8:40 · 306 Gegner"). Press **„End run“** (called „Aufgeben“ until version 6) or let the time run out and the run ends. Online with a friend nearby you are downed instead of the game stopping (Section 13), and the same bar lets you use the Second Chance while you wait for a rescue; if a friend rescues you first, the Second Chance stays armed for later.

These stack with your character's base stats and any in-run passives you pick — they're permanent, applied at the start of every run regardless of character.

## 11. Fortschritt & Speichern (Saving)

- **Between runs:** pearls, Shop levels, achievements (unlocked and claimed), unlocked stages, best stats, and character unlocks save automatically to the browser's local storage every time they change, and persist across sessions once this is live on your actual site. If the browser blocks local storage (private mode, some embeds), the main menu shows a warning, because progress then only lasts until the tab is closed.
- **Mid-run:** the game also snapshots your *current* run — level, weapons, passives, bubble-ring wear, HP, elapsed time, pearls so far, position — whenever the tab is backgrounded, right before the page is hidden, and every ~8 seconds while playing. Since version 5 the snapshot also holds **the scene**: every enemy with its HP and state (including a boss in the middle of its fight and champions), every pickup on the ground (orbs, pearls, hearts, chests), the spawn, lava, Elite and Piranha-Ring timers and your invulnerability. If the browser discards or reloads the tab (common on mobile after being away a while), the main menu offers a **"Weiterspielen"** button and you continue exactly where you were: the game shows a **3-second countdown** in which everything stands still, then it goes on. Only the short-lived things — shots in flight, lava pools, slam circles and shockwaves — are not restored. If you were on the level-up screen, you come back to exactly that screen with the same cards and no upgrade is lost. Reloading is therefore no escape from a bad situation any more: the enemies, the boss's HP and your HP are all as before. (Snapshots from before version 5 still work the old way: no scene, 1.5 s of protection.) **Online runs** are saved too, with their room, and come back with "Online weiterspielen" (Section 13); their pearls are banked when the run ends, you leave the room, or the saved run is no longer used.
- **"Zum Menü" keeps the run (version 6):** the pause menu's **Zum Menü** no longer ends your run. It saves it (with the scene, as above) and goes to the main menu **without asking**; **Weiterspielen** continues it, online runs with **Online weiterspielen** (Section 13). Only when you are downed or the Second Chance bar is open does Zum Menü end the run (pearls banked), as before.
- **Nothing is thrown away:** using **Neustart** in the pause menu and starting a new dive while an old snapshot is waiting bank the pearls and stats of the run they replace — including the Lucky Blobi level check — exactly as dying does. **Neustart always asks once more** ("Wirklich neu starten?", a second tap within 4 s confirms), except in the first 3 seconds of a run. (Until version 5 both Neustart and Zum Menü asked only after the first minute, so sometimes they asked and sometimes not; now the rule is simple: Neustart asks, Zum Menü never needs to because nothing is lost.)
- **Tab switching:** the game pauses automatically when the tab or window loses focus, and it clears any held keys or touch so your fish doesn't keep drifting when you come back.
- **Version 7.17:** the save has a field `tips = { runStart }` that counts how often the run-start hint was shown (default 0; it stops after 2).

### Konto (version 7.2, optional)

Main menu **👤 Konto**. Guests play as before (save in the browser); an account keeps the save on the server. (Version 7.4: the main menu shows Upgrades, Achievements, Boss Album and Account as four tiles, "How to play" sits next to the sound button, and the menu shrinks (smaller mascot, no tagline) on low windows, so it fits without a scroll bar. The `/admin` page is in English.)
- **Create account:** a private login name (3–24 letters, digits, `. _ -`), a public display name (same rules as online names, unique, changeable **once per day**) and a password of at least **8** characters (any characters, emoji count as one; common passwords, game words, the names themselves and passwords known from data leaks are refused; the screen suggests three random words). The browser's save moves into the account. A **recovery code** (16 characters, `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) is shown **once**; "Weiter" only after ticking "Ich habe ihn aufgeschrieben".
- **Log in** on another device: "Stay logged in" (on by default) keeps the device logged in (90 days without use, one year at most). If that browser has guest progress, both saves are shown side by side and you pick one (asked twice); the other stays **30 days** ("Alten Spielstand wiederherstellen").
- **Two devices:** the one that plays last is active; the other stops with "Your account ist gerade auf einem anderen Gerät aktiv" and **Continue here** (no password; the save reloads from the server first). Changes upload 2 s after they happen, each with the next save number; offline (or on a server error) they wait and retry every 30 s. **Version 7.5:** a change that is still waiting goes out at once when the page is hidden or closed (phone locks, tab closes; `keepalive` request), a change made while an upload is running is sent right after it, and **when you come back to an open page** (tab or window in front again, only in the menus) the page uploads what waits and then fetches the newest save, so an upgrade bought on the other device shows up without a reload (an open shop, Blobi, area, achievement or album screen is drawn again). Before 7.5 an open page only looked at the server when it loaded. **Buying on a device that is not the active one** (shop upgrade, Blobi) first takes the account over and loads its save, then asks you to tap again; before, the purchase was uploaded, refused ("inactive") and thrown away by "Continue here". **Version 7.7:** the strategy base is part of the account save (`strategyBase`, the same upload, 64 KB limit; the server checks only its shape): log in on another device and the base is there. A base in the account wins over the one in the browser when it is not older; as a guest the base stays in the browser.
- **Wrong passwords:** after 5 wrong tries a wait of 1 minute, doubling up to 15 minutes. About 30 login tries per minute and 20 sign-ups per hour per network address. Every failed login says the same: "Login name oder Password stimmt nicht."
- **Password vergessen?:** login name + recovery code + new password; you get a new code and every other device is logged out. **Create new code** (with the password) replaces the code; **Change password** logs out the other devices; **Devices** lists them (remove one, or all); **Log out** leaves this browser as a guest with an empty save; **Delete account** (with the password) removes everything.
- **Online rooms** use the account's display name (sent through a one-time ticket valid 30 s, never the login token).
- **The server checks every save**: shop levels, Blobis and achievements must exist, at most 40,000 pearls more than the last save, and everything bought must fit the pearls ever earned plus achievement rewards (+2,000 slack). A refused save is kept for the admin to look at.

## 12. Steuerung (Controls)

- **Desktop:** WASD or arrow keys to move. ESC or the pause icon to pause (holding ESC no longer flickers the pause on and off). **Space** or **Enter** uses the Second Chance while its bar is shown.
- **Mobile:** drag anywhere on the screen to move. The first finger down owns the stick; extra fingers or a resting palm are ignored, and lifting a different finger doesn't stop you. If you lift the steering finger while another finger is still down, that finger takes over the stick (version 6). The stick grows with the screen (radius 11 % of the shorter side, between 46 and 76 px), so it suits phones and tablets alike. **Multi-touch:** buttons that matter during play — the level-up cards of the bottom bar, the Second Chance buttons, the Swarm Call buttons, the 💬 and 📯 buttons, the quick-message wheel and the room pill — react to a second finger while the first keeps steering. Since version 6 they react as soon as the finger touches them (touch / pointer down, not only on the browser's click, which some phones drop while another finger is on the screen), and the bars at the bottom let touches through everywhere except on their buttons, so you can steer under them.
- Attacks on every weapon are fully automatic — the only inputs are movement and which upgrade to pick on level-up (a click/tap, or the keys **1–4**).
- **Online:** **Q** opens the quick-message wheel (keys 1–8 pick a message while it's open), **Tab** shows the player list; on touch screens the 💬 button and a tap on the room pill do the same. As Lead Fish, **R** (or the 📯 button) sends the Swarm Call (the call bar now shows the keys **F** and **N** on its buttons, version 7.4); when a call comes, **F** follows and **N** declines (Section 13).
- Typing in the name or code field of the "Play online" screen never moves the fish or pauses the game.
- **HUD hints (version 7.17):** in your first 2 runs (single player and online, not the strategy base, not a resumed run) a line "Zieh oder WASD zum Schwimmen · Überlebe bis zum Boss bei 5:00" (touch screens: "Zieh zum Schwimmen · …") fades in and goes after 8 s of run time or 50 units of swimming. In the last 60 s before every boss a red chip "Boss in 0:59" sits next to the ring chip (online with friends: the room's boss clock; the chip is gone while the boss warning or the boss is up).

## 13. Play online (Beta)

Play together with others: in a **public room** with everyone who is online ("Quick play") or in a **private room** with your friends, either in **CO-OP** (together against the sea) or in **PvP** — **team PvP** (team Blau against team Rot: your weapons also hit the other team) or, since version 7, **Free-for-all** (free-for-all: no teams, everyone is your enemy). PvP has far fewer enemies. Everyone plays their own run — own level, XP, weapons, HP and pearls — but you share one map: you see each other, fight the same enemies, race for the same loot and rescue each other. The game needs the small online server from the folder `blobfisch-online` (its README explains the free Cloudflare setup); until its address is entered in `index.html` (`ONLINE_SERVER`), the online screens say the server isn't set up yet. Single-player is completely unaffected.

**Getting in (version 7)** — short steps, never more than two choices at once. A bar at the top shows the step ("Schritt 2 von 4"); the line "You play as …" repeats what you have already chosen; your last choice is preselected with a green "Last time" tag (first time: CO-OP is marked "Best to start").
1. Main menu → **🌐 Play online**: your **profile**. A name is already filled in (3–13 characters: letters including umlauts, digits, space, `-` and `_`; 🎲 picks a random one like "Blobi482"); pick your Blobi (one you own). The top line shows how many players are online right now (refreshed every 10 s while the online screens are open). **Weiter**.
2. **Wie willst du spielen?** — **🤝 Together (CO-OP)** or **⚔️ Against each other (PvP)**. Above them **🔄 Weiterspielen** appears when a saved online run is waiting (it says how many minutes it still waits before it's gone). Below them: **"🔑 Du hast einen Code von einem Freund? Join here"** — type the code (upper or lower case); you get that room's mode.
3. Only for PvP: **Welches PvP?** — **🆚 Team vs team** (Blau against Rot) or **🗡️ Free-for-all**.
4. **Mit wem?** — **⚡ Quick play** ("Recommended", with the number of players in this mode right now) or **👥 With friends** (a private room).
5. **Wo willst du spielen?** — the area. For Quick play the area where most players of your mode are right now is preselected (if you have unlocked it) and each area shows how many are there; the button says how many are waiting ("Los geht’s! · 3 warten"). You land in the **fullest public room of that area and mode that still has space**, e.g. "Coral Reef #1" (CO-OP), "Coral Reef PvP #1" (team PvP) or "Coral Reef FFA #1" (Free-for-all), up to **24 players**; when all of them are full a new one opens ("#2"). A public room closes 60 s after its last player has left. For **With friends** you get a **private room** (up to **8 players**) in your mode; your run starts at once, and the room's **code** (4 letters, never I, L or O) is shown in a message, in the room pill under the XP bar (`🤝 KRBX · 👥 1/8`; ⚔️ team PvP, 🗡️ Free-for-all) and in the pause menu. Give it to your friends. The room stays open for 10 minutes after the last player has left, so you can rejoin with the same code.

You play in the room's area, even if you haven't unlocked it yourself yet (beating a boss there still counts normally).

**Free-for-all (version 7, mode `ffa`):** like team PvP (30 % damage, at most a quarter of the target's max HP per hit, far fewer enemies, more XP, knockouts give XP and 30 points), but without teams: every other player is a target, nobody can rescue anyone (0 HP ends your run unless you use the Second Chance), and there is **no Swarm Call**. The best player still wears the crown — every player hunts him, and knocking him out is worth 60 points. Every player keeps their own colour ring.

Names are checked by the server; a name that isn't allowed is replaced by a random one and you're told so. Old random names "Blubbi…" become "Blobi…" automatically. **Versions:** a room only mixes players with the same game version (protocol 5 since version 7; protocol 4 was version 6); an older page gets "Bitte lade die Seite neu", and Quick play keeps versions apart. The server must be updated before the new page goes online.

**The Harbor** — a safe harbour in the middle of every room (radius 250, a dashed circle with an ⚓ sign). Every online run starts inside it with **5 s of protection**. Inside the Harbor enemies can't reach you (they stop at its edge), enemy shots vanish, you take no damage and no lava zones appear — but **your weapons rest** (no shots, the Bubble Ring doesn't hurt, your Schwarm-Freunde don't shoot) and **no enemies spawn** around you. A hint at the bottom of the screen says so while you're inside. Swim out to fight. Outside the Harbor an arrow at the screen edge points back to it with the distance to its edge ("⚓ Harbor · 275 m", version 6), like the arrows to friends.

**What is shared**
- **Seeing each other:** every Blobi shows its name, level and a small HP bar and has its own colour ring. You also see your friends' **Bubble Ring** bubbles, **Schwarm-Freunde** and **shield**; a friend's bubbles block small enemy shots on your screen exactly as in their game. Friends off-screen get a coloured arrow at the screen edge with name and distance (in "m" = 10 world units); the **3 nearest** are shown (5 until version 7). A **downed** friend (in PvP: teammate) always gets a red, pulsing arrow, the **Lead Fish** always gets one (👑), a Lead Fish who is calling a pulsing gold one (📯), and world bosses and champions always get one (red / in the trait's colour), even far away. **Nothing overlaps (version 7):** arrows stay below everything at the top of the screen; when several point the same way the arrows slide apart along the edge and their labels go to the free side; arrows and labels also keep clear of a friend who is just visible at the screen edge. In the Deep-Sea Trench your friends stay visible in the dark.
- **Enemies:** each player's enemies spawn around that player and scale with that player's run time and level, as in single-player. Every enemy chases the **nearest Blobi** — yours or a friend's, but a friend's only while they are within **1,600 units** (about 1.5 screens), so they can always see what chases them. Downed players are never chased; players in the Harbor only when nobody else is in reach (otherwise the enemies wait at the Harbor's edge).
- **Fighting together:** your weapons hit your friends' enemies too; your game predicts the hit at once (the HP bar drops and a predicted kill disappears straight away) and the owner's game confirms it a moment later. Ink-cloud slowing and the Vampire heal work across as well.
- **Loot is shared — first come, first served (version 5):** an enemy drops its loot **once**, where it died, and everyone sees the same orbs, pearls, hearts and chests. Whoever touches a pickup first gets it; it disappears for everyone else. (The owner of the enemy decides who was first; an unconfirmed grab is released after 2.5 s.) Boss hearts and Boss-Schätze are the exception: every player near the boss gets **their own** heart and chest ("einer für jeden"), and a champion drops one chest per player nearby. Loot of a player who leaves, dies or keeps the tab in the background for 15 s is taken over by the others, so nothing vanishes. **Version 6 fixed chests that sometimes gave nothing:** when the owner can't give you a pickup (someone was faster, or you already have your own boss heart or chest), their game answers at once and you get a message ("🎁 Zu spät – jemand anderes hatte die Truhe schon."); a grab that gets no answer leaves the pickup on the ground instead of removing it; a pickup you had grabbed from a player who then left is given to you; and the pickup sound plays only when you really get the item. Each helper's kill counts for their achievements and statistics, and a boss kill opens the next area for every helper. **Version 7.2 fixed pearls that some players never saw:** loot that drops while a friend is 2,400–2,700 units away now reaches them too, and every game resends its whole loot list every **10 s** while a friend is near.
- **More players, more loot:** enemies that spawn near several players are tougher (see below) and drop correspondingly more: XP × the same group factor and a higher pearl chance.
- **Taking damage:** your own game decides when you are hit, checking your Blobi against everything you see: your enemies and your friends', their shots, slam and lava zones, explosion circles and magma waves. A friend's Sea Monster pulls you with its Suction when you are within 900 units (never inside the Harbor). To keep a modified game from knocking you out unfairly, anything that comes from another player's game can take at most **60 % of your max HP in one hit**, its slam and lava circles warn for at least 0.6 s (and stay visible on your screen for at least 0.35 s despite the delay), and a magma wave always starts close to its boss.

**PvP rooms — teams (version 6):** a PvP room has two teams, **🔵 Blau** and **🔴 Rot**. The server puts every new player into the smaller team (and remembers it when you come back); a message tells you your team, and your colour ring, arrow, name and player-list entry use the team colour. In a **private** PvP room you can switch teams in the player list while you are in the Harbor (at most every 5 s). Everything above applies, and in addition every weapon hits the players of the **other team**: **30 % of the damage it would deal to an enemy**, at most **25 % of the victim's max HP per hit** (the Ink Cloud hits players for a flat `(6 + 3 × level) × damage`). Teammates can't hurt each other. Players in the Harbor can't be hit and can't hit others, downed players can't be hit, and the usual i-frames apply. When a player drops to 0 HP, the last player of the other team who hit them within 4 s gets the knockout (both get a message) and **6 XP per level of the knocked-out player**. Being knocked out works like any defeat: Second Chance first, then downed if a **teammate** is near, otherwise the run ends. **Only teammates can rescue you**: you can't revive a player of the other team (their downed ring isn't shown to you).

**PvP has far fewer enemies (version 6):** PvP is about the other team, so each enemy wave is **half as big**, waves come **2.2 × less often** and there are **no Piranha-Rings**. So that you still level up at a similar pace, enemies give **2.5 × XP**. Bosses, world bosses and champions come as usual.
Since version 7.2 the **size and rate of the waves follow the longest run in the room** (each player still spawns around themselves, and the enemies' strength and kinds still follow your own run time), so a newcomer gets as many enemies as a player who has been there for 10 minutes.

**PvP hits (version 7.2):** players of the other team (FFA: everyone) are shown where they are *now* — their newest position, moved forward by the measured round trip to the server (ping every 5 s in PvP) and smoothed over about 40 ms, at most 2.5 × their speed per frame — not 130 ms in the past like friends. Your weapons hit them there. A hit travels with its place and time; the victim's game only counts it if the victim was within **34 units** of that place at that time (the last 0.15 s). Bubbles of the other team stop when they touch you on your own screen.

**Stronger in groups:** while other players are near (active, within 1,600 units), every new enemy that spawns around you gets **+50 % HP per other player** — harder through strength, not more enemies. This includes champions (whose level factor then uses the average level of the players nearby, Section 5). A **world boss** gets **+60 % HP per other player** near it when it appears, and its level factor also uses the average level. Since version 6 bosses and champions also **grow when more players come near later**: their max HP rises to the bigger group's value and they keep their HP fraction (no growth when players leave).

**World bosses and room champions:** the player who has been in the room longest (the "anchor") runs the room's clocks; when they leave, the next one takes over with the same clocks. As long as you are **alone in a room**, everything works exactly like single-player (your boss at 5:00, champions from 4:00). As soon as **others are in the room**, your own boss and champion clocks rest and the room's schedule takes over (it starts from the anchor's own clocks):
- **World boss:** when the room's boss clock runs out (5 minutes of room activity, 4 minutes after the previous world boss is gone), a boss appears near a **random player** who is playing and not in the Harbor. The whole room gets a 3-second warning ("⚠ Ein Weltboss erscheint bei Lena!") and a red arrow. **Every third world boss is a champion boss.** Every player near it when it dies gets their own heart and Boss Treasure, and the kill counts for unlocking the next area for everyone who hit it. Near a world boss fewer normal enemies spawn, as in your own boss fight. The player it appeared at plays it; if they leave the room or their run ends, the nearest other player (within about 3,600 units) **takes it over** with all its HP and state, so a world boss no longer vanishes with its host. Room champions are handed over the same way. Since version 6 the heir may also be a **paused** friend (their game takes the boss and keeps it frozen until they continue), and the up to **40 nearest normal enemies** go along too, so a player dying or pausing no longer makes the boss and every mob vanish. The boss bar shows the boss's name, its encounter number in the room (×2, ×3 …) and its phase; it no longer shows whose boss it is. **When everyone in the room has died** (all on the game-over screen), the room's schedule starts over for the next runs: the first world boss comes after 5 minutes again and counts as ×1 (version 5 kept counting, so after a wipe the next boss was announced as ×4).
- **Room champions:** every 120–150 s (never during a world boss), one champion appears near a random player and is announced to the room ("👑 Champion bei Lena: Flinker Crab"); at most 1 at a time (2 after 15:00 of the anchor's run). Every player nearby gets a chest.

**Downed and rescued:** at 0 HP, if you bought the **Second Chance**, its bar appears (Section 9) — with a friend (PvP: teammate) near you are downed at the same time and can use it or wait for the rescue. Otherwise, if a friend who is playing is within **2,200 units** (about 2 screens), you are **downed for 20 s**: you can't move, attack or collect anything, you take no damage, and enemies ignore you. Your Blobi lies faded inside a red, pulsing ring; a banner shows the seconds left. A friend who stays within **60 units** of you (the dashed circle) for **3 s** rescues you — a green ring fills up on both screens — and you're back with **30 % HP**, 2 s of protection and nearby enemies pushed away, like the Second Chance. Both of you get a message. If no friend is within 2,200 units when you drop to 0 HP, or the 20 s run out, your run ends as in single-player.

**Pause and level-ups while friends are near:** if an active friend is within 2,400 units, **ESC** or the pause button opens the pause menu at the bottom of the screen, but **the game keeps running** (it says so); if no friend is near any more, it becomes a real pause. **Level-up cards** slide in as a bar at the bottom: pick with **1–4** or a tap; after **15 s** the first card is taken automatically. Alone, both stop the game as in single-player. Switching to another window counts as pressing pause. **Tab in the background** always pauses: your friends then see you as sleeping — your Blobi and your enemies disappear for them until you're back (💤 in the player list). If a friend's game is really paused, their enemies stand still on your screen in their normal colours; you can still hit and defeat them (their game applies it while paused), but they don't hurt you.

**Lead Fish & Swarm Call (version 6).** Every room keeps a board of **Swarm points** (swarm points). The best active player wears the crown — the **Lead Fish** (lead fish) — and can call the whole swarm to him.

- **Points:** every enemy you defeat 1, an Elite 3, a champion 20, a boss 50 (a champion boss 70), rescuing a friend 15; in PvP a knockout 30 (60 if you knock out the other team's Lead Fish). The Lead Fish also gets 3 points for every player who follows his call. The room keeps your points while you're away and gives them back when you return (your browser has a private key; the name doesn't matter), and they are saved when the room empties. The server limits how fast points can rise (60 per second, plus 300 at once), so a modified game can't buy the crown.
- **The crown:** the player with the most points becomes Lead Fish once they have at least **100 points** and at least **2 active players** are in the room (PvP: in their team — each team has its own Lead Fish). A challenger needs 10 % more points (at least 25 more) to take the crown, so it doesn't flicker. "Active" means playing and not 60 s without any key or touch; if the Lead Fish is no longer active (or reloads), the crown waits 10 s before moving on. The Lead Fish wears a golden crown above the name on every screen, and a message tells the room when the crown changes.
- **HUD — the score card (version 7):** under the room pill a small card with at most three labelled rows: in team PvP first the score **🔵 1.200 ▬▬▬ 950 🔴** with a bar (your team underlined); then **"🏅 Platz 3 von 5 · 480 Punkte"** (in team PvP "🏅 🔴 Im Team: Platz 2 von 3 · …"); then the crown: "👑 Krone: Lena · 1.250", "👑 Du hast die Krone!" (Free-for-all: "… Alle jagen dich."), "👑 Crown from 100 points" or, while there are too few players, "👑 Crown from 2 players (im Team)". Tapping the card opens the player list; while the list is open the card hides so nothing lies on top of each other. The room pill shows only mode, room and players ("⚔️ KRBX · 👥 3/8"); your team is on the card. **Toasts** in a run always appear just below the card (and the boss bar), never on top of them.
- **Swarm Call (swarm call)** (not in Free-for-all): the Lead Fish gets a 📯 button next to 💬 (key **R**). The first call is possible 60 s after the first crown, then once every **3 minutes** per room (PvP: per team); a new Lead Fish inherits the timer, and the button shows the time left. A call goes to everyone (PvP: only the own team): a horn sounds, a gold bar at the bottom says who calls and how far away ("📯 Lena ruft! · 840 m"), and you have **10 s** to answer with **🐟 Follow** (F) or **Nein** (N). A gold arrow points to the caller meanwhile.
- **Strömungsritt (current ride):** after Follow there is a 0.6 s wind-up (Nein still cancels), then a current carries you to the Lead Fish at 3,000 units per second (1.2–3 s). While riding you can't be hit, but you also don't attack, collect anything or spawn enemies; a trail shows the ride on every screen. Your own enemies that are left more than 2,400 units behind move to the spawn ring around your new spot, so they don't sit somewhere far away. You can't follow while you're downed, the Second Chance bar is open or your own boss fight is on (the bar says why). If you're already within 1,200 units of the Lead Fish you skip the ride and get the bonus at once.
- **Arrival:** you land on a ring 160 units around the Lead Fish. A wave pushes your normal enemies within 220 units out of the circle and **stuns them for 1 s** (no damage; bosses and champions only flash), and you have 1.5 s of protection.
- **Schwarmgeist:** the Lead Fish, everyone who followed and everyone who was already near gets **+15 % damage for 30 s**, the followers while within 900 units of the Lead Fish (a pill in the HUD shows the time; it doesn't stack). Players of the other team get a message that a call went out.
- **Safety:** the server decides who wears the crown and when a call is allowed; a player can only fly the ride flag for at most 4 s after a real call, so nobody can fake rides to dodge hits.

**Quick messages:** **Q** or the 💬 button opens a wheel with 8 messages — 👋 Hallo, 🆘 Hilfe!, 📍 Hierher!, 🙏 Danke!, ⚠️ Boss!, 🎉 GG, 👑 Champion!, 👍 OK — pick with a click/tap or the keys 1–8 while the wheel is open. The message shows as a speech bubble above your Blobi for 3 s; at most one every 2 s. Players who can't see you get it as a message at the top ("💬 Lena: Hilfe!"). There is no free text chat.

**Player list:** **Tab** or a tap on the room pill shows everyone in the room with colour, Blobi, name, level, **Swarm points** (👑 for the Lead Fish) and what they're doing (distance, ⚓ Harbor, ⏸ Pause, 💔 Hilfe!, 💤 schläft, ☠ vorbei). In team-PvP rooms it is split into the two teams with their point totals, and in private team-PvP rooms it has the team-switch button. A line at the bottom explains the points for the room's mode. It opens just below the room pill.

**Game over, leaving, being away:** your run ends as usual (summary, pearls banked) and your enemies vanish from the others' screens. **"Dive again"** starts a new run at level 1 in the Harbor of the same room. **"Zum Menü"** on the game-over screen leaves the room; **"Zum Menü" in the pause menu** leaves the room but **keeps your run** (version 6): it is saved and "Online weiterspielen" brings you back into the same room (for 30 minutes), as after a reload. After **5 minutes without any key, click, touch or movement** you leave the room automatically and continue alone (your place in the room is free again). Holding the touch stick and swimming count as activity (until version 4 a phone player who steered for 5 minutes without lifting the thumb was taken out of the room by mistake).

**Reloading the page:** online runs are saved every ~8 seconds like single-player runs, together with their room. After a reload the main menu shows **"🌐 Online weiterspielen · Coral Reef #1 · Lv 12 · 8:30"** (the same button is on the "Wie willst du spielen?" screen). It takes you back into the same room with your level, XP, weapons, passives, HP, pearls and time. If the run was saved less than 60 s ago you continue **at the same spot with your enemies and pickups** (after the 3-second countdown); after longer you start in the Harbor with 5 s protection. Joining the same room with its code or through Quick play restores the run too. If the private room no longer exists, you continue in Quick play of the same area. A saved online run is kept for 30 minutes; starting a new online run elsewhere ends it — in both cases its pearls are banked. If you were downed when the page closed, the run counts as over (pearls banked).

**Connection problems:** if the connection drops, the game keeps running alone and reconnects by itself (up to 4 tries); if that fails, you continue alone (and the run can be resumed like a single-player run). Messages explain wrong codes ("gibt es nicht"), full rooms (Quick play simply finds another room), an unreachable server, **"Der Online-Modus ist für heute voll"** (the server's free daily limit is used up; it resets at 00:00 UTC, which is 1:00 or 2:00 at night in Switzerland — the message shows the local time) and an outdated page ("Bitte lade die Seite neu").

**Technical numbers** (for tuning): the game sends its state **10 times per second** while another player is within 2,400 units (or one of your enemies is near them), **6.7 per second** when more than 6 players are on one screen and **5 per second** with more than 12; **once per second** when everyone is far away, every 2 s while paused or on the game-over screen, and **nothing at all while you're alone in the room** (the room then sleeps and costs nothing). Friends and their enemies are shown in the past so they move smoothly: at least 130 ms, in a crowd the time between their messages plus 30 ms, and (version 7.4) plus 0.9 times the largest lateness of their recent messages (a peak that fades over about 5 s), at most 420 ms (`NET_DELAY_MAX`). The delay follows that target gradually (deeper by up to 350 ms per second, shallower by 80 ms per second) and what is drawn glides: the normal movement of the sampled position is followed one to one, a jump (a late message, the end of the 150 ms extrapolation, a correction by the owner) is caught up within about 100 ms, jumps above 300 units are immediate. A friend who sends nothing for 3.5 s counts as asleep. Values from other players are capped (damage per hit 1,000 and at most 60 % of your max HP, PvP hits 25 %, XP per kill 1,000). The owner repeats the complete list of its loot every 5 s (`PK_RESEND`, version 7.4; was 10 s), and a loot number reported "gone" counts again after 8 s (a new run or a reload starts counting from 1). Every friend has his own bit for "has received this enemy completely" (7.4; it was the id modulo 30, so after 30 joins two friends shared one). A message is about 0.5–1.5 KB. Measured: two players together send about 10 messages and 5–10 KB per second each; four players together about 14 KB/s up and 41 KB/s down each; 24 players on one screen about 82 KB/s down each (bot test against the real server software). ## 14. Grafik, Klang & Leistung (Graphics, sound and performance)

- **Same view on every device:** the game zooms so that every screen shows about the same amount of world (about 1.3 million square units, e.g. 1,520 × 855 units on a 16:9 screen). Phones show everything a little smaller, big monitors a little larger (zoom limited to 0.7 – 2), so neither enemy spawns, weapon reach nor difficulty depend on your screen size any more. Name tags, champion tags, speech bubbles and the Harbor sign are drawn at a fixed size on small screens, so they stay readable.
- **Graphics levels (version 7.6, replaces the 7.4 resolution steps):** six levels, 0 = hoch … 5 = einfach. Each sets the canvas resolution, the sharpness of boss pictures, how often boss art is redrawn, whether bosses have the glowing outline, and how many sparks and background things are drawn:

  | Level | Resolution (× screen density, 0.6 – 2 px per CSS px) | Boss pictures (× what the screen shows) | Boss art redrawn | Boss outline | Sparks / scenery |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0 hoch | 1.5 (never above 2 px per CSS px) | 1.0, up to 2 px per unit and 1,536 px wide | every frame | yes | 100 % |
  | 1 (start, as 7.4) | 1.0 | 1.0, at most 1.5 px per unit, 1,024 px wide | every 2nd | yes | 100 % |
  | 2 | 0.8 | 0.9 | every 2nd | yes | 85 % |
  | 3 | 0.65 | 0.75 | every 3rd | no (and no deep glows) | 65 % |
  | 4 | 0.5 (not below 0.6 px per CSS px) | 0.6 | every 4th | no | 50 % |
  | 5 einfach | 0.5 (same floor) | 0.5 | every 6th | no | 35 % |

  Boss pictures are now baked as fine as the screen shows them (canvas resolution × zoom), no finer than 7.4 did: on a normal monitor that is about 0.85 px per unit instead of 1, and they shrink with the level (a legend picture costs about 30 ms to redraw without a graphics card at 1,000 px, about 12 ms at 400 px).
  **Automatic (default):** every second it compares the frames shown with what the screen can show (60 Hz: under 75 % = 45 fps is slow, over 95 % = 57 fps is smooth). Two slow seconds take one level off (a second under 50 % already counts double; under 40 % it drops two levels). Two frames over 0.35 s within 20 s drop two levels at once, with a note once the level is 4 or 5. After 12 smooth seconds it climbs one level; if it has to drop again within 90 s of the last drop the wait doubles (12 → 24 → 48 → 96 → at most 120 s) and shrinks by a quarter with every climb. **Level 0 (hoch)** is only entered from level 1 when, after the smooth seconds, a frame costs on average under 5 ms of work and the screen does not run at half rate (so a machine with plenty of room draws a finer, crisper picture and redraws bosses every frame; a machine that only just reaches 60 never gets there). The automatic never starts on level 0; it starts on the level it last settled on (saved on this device, at least 1).
- **Boss drawing (version 7.4):** a boss keeps one finished picture (at most 1,024 pixels wide, 1,536 on level 0; version 7.6: baked as fine as the screen shows it and following the graphics level, see above); the scratch surfaces for the drawing, the silhouette and the outline are shared by all bosses of the same size (a sea serpent's segments share them) and are freed after 4 s without use. The outline is computed at half (legends: about a third) of the resolution with 4 diagonally shifted copies (champion bosses: 8 plus 8). Bosses are only skipped when their whole body and aura are off screen; bosses redraw in different frames so the load is even (7.6: every 1st – 6th frame by level). Memory for a stress scene of 31 bosses at 2 pixels per unit: 40 canvases and about 32 MB (was 76 canvases and 110 MB).
- **A failing draw call no longer ruins the picture (version 7.4):** if one drawing function throws an error, the game resets the drawing state and carries on with the next layer / enemy / shot / particle instead of dropping the rest of the frame (before, enemies, pearls and the player could be missing for that frame and the next frame could be drawn shifted or cut off).
- **The graphics setting (versions 7.4 / 7.6):** *Pause → Grafik* cycles **automatisch → einfach → hoch**. *Einfach* keeps level 5 and *hoch* level 0 for good (no automatic change); *automatisch* is the governor above. The choice is saved on this device (a setting from before 7.6, "normal", counts as automatisch); `?gfx=safe` and `?gfx=high` force einfach / hoch for one visit. (Before 7.6 `?gfx=safe` did nothing: a stray character in its test never matched.) The HUD capsules have no `backdrop-filter` any more, the canvas is opaque (`alpha: false`), and the run snapshot (every 8 s) is saved when the browser is idle.
- **Even frame pacing (version 6):** fast screens (90 or 120 Hz, e.g. the Realme 6) show a new picture every 11 or 8 ms. A phone that can't draw that fast drops frames irregularly, which looks choppier than an even, lower rate. The game notices the missed frames and then draws only every second frame (an even 45 or 60 frames per second; the game logic still runs every frame). After a long calm stretch it tries the full rate again, waiting longer each time it has to step back. 60 Hz screens are not affected.
- **Glows without blur:** every glow (shots, bubbles, Blobis, the shield, companions, bosses' auras) is a pre-drawn gradient instead of the browser's `shadowBlur`, which is very slow on weak phones. Enemies, bosses and projectiles are pre-drawn once and then only copied.
- **Art — simple where it is background, detailed where it matters:** the backgrounds and normal enemies keep the calm, minimalist look (soft colour gradient, dot grid, faint scenery). Detailed art is used for what you should notice: **champions** (animated drawings, Section 5), **bosses** (outlines, animations, bigger, Section 4), and the parts of the world that do something: **lava pools** (smooth glowing magma without an outline, bubbling and cooling), **kelp patches** (the slowing areas: dense, swaying kelp with leaves and floats and a visible rim), and the **pickups** (XP cores, pearls, hearts, chests). Shots have shapes: the Bubble Shot is a bubble, the piercing shot a water drop, the Coral Spear a spear, the companions' shots small glowing orbs. The Swarm Buddy companions are small mint-green blobs, like the player's own style.
- **Fonts:** Baloo 2 and Nunito are embedded in `index.html` (cut down to the characters the game uses, about 95 KB, plus Nunito's Cyrillic letters for Ukrainian, about 58 KB), so the game makes no request to Google Fonts and looks the same offline.
- **Sound (version 6, reworked in version 7):** the sounds are small synthesizer voices instead of simple beeps — bubbles, splashes, bells, a horn for the Swarm Call, a whoosh and a current for the ride. Everything runs through a compressor, so loud moments don't distort; each voice has an envelope, many glide in pitch and get a little randomness, so repeated sounds don't sound mechanical. Frequent sounds are throttled, and at most 28 voices play at once. **Version 7:** a short underwater reverb gives the sounds space; your own hits make a quiet tick; collecting orbs and pearls quickly one after another climbs a scale (up to two octaves, the chain resets after 0.38 s); quick kills in a row sound brighter each time (up to +7 semitones, reset after 0.65 s; Elites thump more); defeating a boss or champion has its own big sound (boom, rising chord, sparkle); a PvP knockout has a punchy two-note sting; buying in the Shop or the Blobi selection jingles; the level-up chord is fuller; menu buttons make a soft bubble and going one step further in the online menus two rising notes. The 🔊 button in the menu switches sound on and off; in the pause menu the button says **"🔊 Ton: an" / "🔇 Ton: aus"**.

## 16. Sprachen (Languages)

- **Toggle:** the start menu has a well-visible language switch at the top: **🌐 DE · EN · UA** (German, English, Ukrainian). The choice is saved with your progress. On the first visit the game follows the browser: German for German browsers, Ukrainian for Ukrainian ones, English for everything else.
- **What is translated:** everything you see — menus, the Shop, Blobis, areas, achievements, the instructions, the upgrade cards with their numbers, messages, the HUD, boss and champion names (champion names are built per language, e.g. "Swift, Armored Crab" / "Швидкий, броньований краб"), the pause, Second Chance and game-over screens, and all online screens and room messages. Numbers follow the language (2,5 / 2.5 / 2,5; 1.000 / 1,000 / 1 000), and so do the units (s / с, m / м). In English the Harbor is the **Harbor**, the Lead Fish the **Lead Fish**, the Swarm Call the **Swarm Call**.
- **For changes:** the game code keeps writing German. A translation layer turns each text into the chosen language when it reaches the screen: page text (watched with a MutationObserver), text drawn on the canvas (`fillText`, `strokeText`, `measureText`) and messages. The dictionary `I18N_DATA` holds rows of `[German, English, Ukrainian]`; `{}` marks a number or name that is filled in. A new German text without a row stays German in EN and UA, so every new text needs a row. `webtest/browser_i18n.js` visits every screen in all languages and lists any text that still looks German; `I18N_LANG=en node webtest/net_checks.js` does the same for online messages.
- This guide keeps the German in-game names (Bubble Ring, Harbor, Lead Fish …) because they are the names in the code and in the German version.
